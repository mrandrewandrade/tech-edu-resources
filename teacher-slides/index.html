General Slides

Reusable classroom decks across subjects and learning environments

Search reusable classroom slides across mathematics, science, business, technology, arts, human well-being, and other learning environments.

Reusable slide decks and lightweight classroom activities for educators and learners across subjects including mathematics, science, business, arts, and technological education. Source files are organized in the Teaching folder.

Number Systems contains significant figures, powers of ten, notation, and related measurement material. Well-Being contains Funday Friday activities, reflection, creativity, attention, and connection material. General holds reusable templates and process decks that do not belong to one of those collections.

Filters are saved in the page URL as you select them, so you can copy the address bar and send someone the same filtered view.
