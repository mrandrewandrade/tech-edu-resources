General Materials

Reusable assignments, worksheets, handouts, and worked examples

Search and filter reusable classroom assignments and teaching materials across technological education, human well-being, and cross-curricular learning.

Reusable classroom materials for students and teachers. Generated PDFs link directly to their canonical files at andrewandrade.ca/assets, avoiding duplicate copies. Editable classroom resources and current TAS design-process examples link to their student-facing Google Docs in the Teaching folder.

The library intentionally links to generic teaching materials, not student submissions or student copies. General materials are broadly reusable across subjects and technology courses. Well-Being materials focus on identity, reflection, listening, goal setting, community, and related learning routines.

Filters are saved in the page URL as you select them, so you can copy the address bar and send someone the same filtered view.
