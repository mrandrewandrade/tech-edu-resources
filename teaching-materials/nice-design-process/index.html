N.I.C.E.Design Process

Needs · Inquiry · Communicate · Evaluate

Start with the real situation and what people need. Investigate what already exists. Communicate a clear idea, gather useful feedback, build a workable plan, and evaluate what should change next.

Teach the N.I.C.E. curriculum Open the slides Open completed examples
