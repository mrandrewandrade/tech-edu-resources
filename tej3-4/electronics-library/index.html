Required Published

D01 Digital inputs and stable switches

Required pre-microcontroller sequence with authoritative circuits, state tables, debouncing, compatibility, and a logic-system project.

Main Quest Side Quests Published

CS01-CS08 Basic Computer Systems

Fast electricity bridge, safe opening, power connections, first boot, firmware, Xubuntu 26.04 LTS, RAM upgrade, benchmarking, a separate recovery pilot, and six optional service extensions.
