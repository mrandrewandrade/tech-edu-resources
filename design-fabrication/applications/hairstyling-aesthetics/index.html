Hairstyling & Aesthetics Project Ideas

TXJ2O / TXJ3E / TXJ4E · workstation flow, consultation, and portable service organization

Hairstyling and Aesthetics projects for organizers, displays, sectioning aids, and portable work modules.

Hairstyling and Aesthetics applications should improve consultation, organization, ergonomics, and workflow while respecting infection-prevention practices. Fabricated classroom prototypes do not make a material heat-safe, disinfectable, or suitable for direct client contact.

Important boundary: Keep unverified laser-cut wood, porous prints, and unknown plastics away from hot tools, chemicals, wet service, and items that must be disinfected or sterilized. Follow product instructions, public-health guidance, board policy, and local salon procedures.

Project directions

Consultation colour-ring display

TXJ2O / TXJ3E · Display family

Design a stable system that organizes removable colour samples and supports a clear client conversation.
Process
Consultation observation → grouping study → mock-up → reach/readability test
Skills
Colour organization, labels, ergonomics, client communication
Difficulty / size
Introductory–intermediate · tabletop
Material
Non-service display materials; removable commercial sample rings
Structural
Prevent tipping and sample tangling
Safety
Display only; no chemical mixing, hot-tool, or sanitized-service use
Library
Display and tag components
Lesson
Layout, Type & Colour
Extension
Create an inclusive consultation guide for varied hair characteristics

Dry-tool workstation organizer

TXJ2O · Organizer family

Organize clean, dry, room-temperature practice items so inventory and workflow are visible.
Process
Inventory → grip/reach measurement → cardboard trial → dry-use prototype
Skills
Workflow, clearance, labels, centre of mass, cleaning assessment
Difficulty / size
Intermediate · bounded desktop module
Material
Prototype material separated from disinfection-required items
Structural
Wide base; no wall load without approved mounting
Safety
No hot irons, blades, wet implements, chemicals, or sterilization claims
Library
Relevant dry-tool rack forms
Lesson
Tool Holder
Extension
Redesign the geometry in a verified non-porous material

Portable consultation module

TXJ3E / TXJ4E · Portable work module

Create a compact module for forms, reference images, clean sample materials, and privacy-conscious note taking.
Process
Service journey → information inventory → privacy review → prototype → setup test
Skills
Client-centred design, compartments, handles, visual communication
Difficulty / size
Intermediate · lap or tabletop scale
Material
Wipeable commercial case with fabricated non-clinical dividers
Structural
Retain contents during transport; avoid exposed fasteners
Safety
Do not store chemicals or personal client records in an unsecured prototype
Library
Modular faces and dividers
Lesson
Needs & Necessities
Extension
Conduct a timed setup and revise the sequence

Curriculum context: Relate the project to consultation, salon routines, ergonomics, professional communication, infection prevention, safe product/tool use, environmental choices, and sector careers in the current TXJ course.
