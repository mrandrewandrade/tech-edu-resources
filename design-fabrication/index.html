General Design & Fabrication

Design ideas, communicate them, fabricate them, test them, and improve them.

Free General Design & Fabrication lessons and project ideas for Ontario Broad-Based Technological Education, organized by fabrication method and technology area.

Ontario Broad-Based Technological Education · Reusable curriculum

This open pathway helps students move from a real need to a communicated idea, a measured prototype, and an improved fabricated result. It connects design with digital communication and fabrication without treating any one technology area—or any one machine—as the default context.

The fabrication method is transferable. The application changes with the technology area.

N.I.C.E. Design Process

Understand the situation, distinguish necessities from nice-to-haves, investigate existing solutions, generate alternatives, communicate a concept, prototype, evaluate, and begin the next iteration.

Digital Design & Communication

Reusable Ontario technology resources

These lessons can support Ontario secondary school Broad-Based Technological Education in different ways. Teachers can use the whole sequence or select a design process, communication, measurement, or fabrication lesson that fits a local project, technology context, and available equipment.

Ontario curriculum relevance: The complete pathway is especially useful for TIJ1O Exploring Technologies. Individual units also transfer to TDJ, TMJ, TGJ, TEJ, TCJ, THJ, TTJ, TXJ, TFJ, and TPJ contexts where the learning genuinely involves design, measurement, prototyping, communication, or fabrication. The application pages make those connections explicit without claiming one-to-one curriculum expectation mappings.
