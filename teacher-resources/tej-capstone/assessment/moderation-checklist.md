# Assessment Moderation Checklist

Use before finalizing results across different project choices.

## Evidence comparability

- [ ] All projects address design, safety, Linux/Python, measurements/reasoning, troubleshooting, revision, communication, and individual explanation.
- [ ] Extensions do not compensate for missing core evidence.
- [ ] Simpler projects can demonstrate high-level reasoning and testing.
- [ ] A working product is not being scored as automatic mastery.
- [ ] Equipment failure is not being treated as student failure.
- [ ] Team evidence is not automatically attributed to every member.

## Quality of judgement

- [ ] The most consistent and recent evidence is considered.
- [ ] Observations, conversations, and products are represented where available.
- [ ] Comments identify technical evidence rather than personality or compliance.
- [ ] Similar performance descriptions result in similar levels across project types.
- [ ] Accommodations change access/format, not the intended learning.

## Documentation

- [ ] Criterion levels are supported by dated evidence.
- [ ] Any missing individual evidence has a follow-up plan.
- [ ] Partly unsuccessful projects include explicit diagnostic evidence.
- [ ] Student privacy and approved display identity are maintained.
- [ ] Teacher keys, private notes, and inventory/security information are not in the public student site.
